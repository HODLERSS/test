NVIDIA's strategy rests on two reinforcing pillars: vertically integrated hardware and horizontally open software
HARDWARE STRATEGY
AI Factory as Product
From chips to rack-scale systems to gigawatt facilities
  • Vera Rubin system: 7 chips, 5 rack types, 100% liquid cooled, 2-hour install vs. 2-day prior
  • Groq integration: Disaggregated inference pairs high-throughput Rubin with low-latency Groq SRAM
  • DSX platform: Digital twin for designing and operating AI factories at gigawatt scale
SOFTWARE STRATEGY
Open Models + Agent Stack
From CUDAx libraries to NemoClaw enterprise agents
  • 1,000+ CUDAx libraries: cuDF for structured data, cuVS for vectors, 100 new libraries announced at GTC
  • 6 open model families: Nemotron, Cosmos, GROOT, Alpamayo, BioNeMo, Earth-2 all at frontier
  • NemoClaw: Enterprise-secure agent reference design with policy engine and privacy router

Disaggregated inference, constrained Vera Rubin supply, and OpenClaw's rapid adoption will force infrastructure decisions by Q3
Inference
Architecture
  • Disaggregated inference (prefill on GPU, decode on SRAM) is now NVIDIA's endorsed architecture, not a fringe idea
  • Teams running homogeneous GPU clusters should evaluate heterogeneous prefill/decode splits for 35x gains
  • KV cache management will become the critical bottleneck as context windows grow to millions of tokens
Capacity
Planning
  • $1T demand through 2027 means GPU allocation will remain constrained. Spot pricing will keep rising.
  • Vera Rubin's 350x improvement changes unit economics: plan hardware refreshes around H2 2026 timeline
  • Huang's 75/25 GPU/LPU allocation guidance gives a concrete starting point for mixed-silicon planning
Platform
Strategy
  • NVIDIA's CUDAx + open models + NemoClaw stack is a full-stack play that competes with your platform layer
  • OpenClaw adoption rate signals that the agent runtime standard may already be decided. Adapt or integrate.
  • Confidential computing and sovereign deployment are the new unlock for regulated enterprise revenue

Rethink
Inference
  • Evaluate disaggregated prefill/decode architecture against your current homogeneous GPU clusters
  • Model your inference fleet as a tokens-per-watt factory with tiered pricing ($3 to $150/M tokens)
  • Benchmark your current cost/token against Vera Rubin's 350x curve to time your hardware refresh
Own the
Agent Layer
  • Decide whether to build on, compete with, or integrate OpenClaw before the standard solidifies
  • NVIDIA's NemoClaw is a reference, not a lock-in. The policy engine is where your differentiation lives.
  • Agent workloads will 10x inference demand per user. Plan capacity for reasoning + tool-use token volume.
Secure
Capacity
  • Vera Rubin allocation is already constrained. First rack running on Azure. Lock in your position now.
  • Plan your networking topology: copper NVLink-72, optical NVLink-576, or CPO. NVIDIA is shipping all three.
  • Confidential computing unlocks regulated enterprise revenue that open-cloud deployments cannot reach.
